
# KrishiDrishti (कृषि दृष्टि) 🌾

> Empowering farmers with intelligent crop monitoring, precision advisory, and data-driven agricultural insights.

---

## 📌 Overview

**KrishiDrishti** is a smart agriculture web and mobile platform designed to bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern precision agriculture. By integrating crop analytics, weather-based advisories, and direct market/soil insights, KrishiDrishti helps farmers make timely, informed decisions to enhance yield and reduce resource wastage.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Crop Health & Disease Detection:** Upload leaf images to diagnose common crop diseases with treatment recommendations.
- **Micro-Climate Weather Advisory:** Hyperlocal forecasts and timely alerts for irrigation, pest risk, and harvesting windows.
- **Soil & Fertilizer Recommendation:** Input soil parameters (NPK, pH) to receive balanced fertilizer and nutrient schedules.
- **Market Price Intelligence (Mandi Insights):** Real-time commodity tracking across regional mandis to ensure fair pricing.
- **Multilingual Support:** Accessible UI with regional language support to ensure ease of use for grassroots farming communities.
